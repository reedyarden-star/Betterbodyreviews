Editorial standards

How we review

Trust is everything for a site like this. Here's exactly how we research, write, and rank.

How we research

  • We use trusted sources: government health agencies, major medical centers, official drug labels, and published studies.
  • For brands, we go straight to the brand's own website for prices, products, and how their service works.
  • We link our sources at the bottom of every article so you can check them yourself.

How we rank brands

When we compare brands, we score them on the same clear rules every time:

  • Price and value: what you really pay, and whether pricing is clear.
  • Care: whether a licensed doctor or clinician is involved.
  • Options: the range of treatments offered.
  • Convenience: how easy it is to start, get help, and get products.
  • Transparency: how open the brand is about pricing, risks, and policies.

We don't invent star ratings or customer reviews. If we didn't verify it, we don't publish it.

How we keep things fresh

Prices and offers change often. Every brand listing shows the date we last checked it, and we review our pages regularly.

How we make money

We may earn money from some of the brands we mention. That never changes our rules or our rankings. Read our advertising disclosure for details.
